The example app

The guides build a Workplace Issue Reporter: a simple app, then an AI skill, then an AI agent. Use it as a model for your own.

The problem

Office problems are reported by email, chat and in the corridor. Reports get lost or duplicated, and people never hear back.

The headline

Anyone can report an office problem in 30 seconds, and it reaches the right team without anyone sorting an inbox.

See it built step by step
App
Alex reports that the heating is broken in meeting room 3B.
AI skill
Suggests a category, a priority and a one-line summary.
AI agent
Flags two similar reports and explains why in the work notes.
App

App
